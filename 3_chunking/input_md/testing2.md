## 2 Model Descriptions

The VENTUS suspended air handling units are draw-thru air handlers for recuperation, cooling or/and heating load conditions of the air flow range 280-4300 m3/h. The VENTUS air-handling units are designed for a ventilation  system  where  an  access  to  the rotating  parts  of  the  unit  (a  fan's  rotor)  is feasible  neither  from  the  overpressure  nor sub atmospheric pressure side of the unit.

AHUs  are  equipped  with  a  wide  range  of functional  sections  which  offers  extensive possibilities  of  realizing  the  air  treatment process starting from the simplest supply and exhaust to conditioning the supplied air in the field of such parameters as temperature (heat recovery, heating:  water  of  electric  heaters, cooling:  water  or  freon  coolers),  filtration, primary and secondary filters as well as noise level  reduction  (we  offer  silencing  curtains without any casing to be assembled inside a duct).
